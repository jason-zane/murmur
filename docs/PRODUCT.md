# Concourse

Concourse brings the day's calendar, Gmail inboxes and meeting notes together on Mac and web. Dictation and meeting capture work on the Mac without an account; a Concourse account enables hosted calendars, Gmail, synchronisation and read-only AI connectors.

People should begin work before learning configuration. Four primary destinations are Today, Calendar, Mail and Notes. Dictation history belongs beside Notes; booking links belong beside Calendar. Connected apps and Settings are utilities. Connections are optional, individually explained and initiated by the person. Provider authorisation never happens implicitly. Outlook is deferred.

The installed Mac app retains its bundle identity, signing requirement, keyboard shortcuts, local notes and capture behaviour. Missing authorisation and connection failures explain the recovery without pretending content is empty or connected. Advanced configuration is disclosed on demand.

## 30 September refinement

The default setup is a connected workspace: sign in to Concourse, then connect Google calendars and Gmail. Offline access is part of this experience; local-only notes/dictation remain a secondary path. Never require microphone/Accessibility permissions to browse Calendar or Mail.

Booking links and Dictation history are permanent peer destinations. Do not conditionally add/remove navigation rows. Calendar opens in Week at 08:00, with pinned day headings, opaque event blocks, a stable toolbar and compact status disclosure. Polling must preserve visible content and scroll position. Time-zone and offline details belong in the status popover or relevant settings.
